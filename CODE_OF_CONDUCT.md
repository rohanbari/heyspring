# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Show empathy toward other contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Public or private abuse of any community member.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct and may take corrective action for unacceptable behavior.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the maintainers through the channels in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
